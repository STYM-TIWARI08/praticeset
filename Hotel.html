About Gangapalace

Nestled along the timeless banks of the Ganga, Gangapalace blends traditional Indian hospitality with modern comfort. Every room is designed to give you a peaceful retreat, while our staff ensures your stay feels personal, warm, and effortless.

From sunrise views over the river to quiet evenings on the terrace, we've built Gangapalace to be more than just a place to stay — it's an experience you'll want to return to. Whether you're here for a short getaway or an extended stay, our doors are always open with genuine warmth and care.
